## KEY TAKEAWAYS
Most connections end up, at some point, going through a [[2025-11-02-Cabled-Connections| cable]].
In the case of twisted copper, several cables in pairs of a positive and a negative, are twisted with different rates to allow the signals to be compared and correctly assessed at the other end of the cable.

Depending on the standard used, the cable will be part of a media or another, all of them following the [[IEEE|802.3 Ethernet Standard]]. They are also described as a **category** of cable, each one following a different number (1,2,3,4, etc.), all of them, including recommendations and minimum requirements, following the **standard**. 


| Type                  | Connection                                                                                                                | Function                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ***Coaxial Cable***   | Several cables are twisted around a shielding. The shielding covers an insulator, which covers the main wire conductor    | Usually used in television or high speed internets                                                 |
| ***Twinaxial Cable*** | Several outer cables are twisted around a shielding. The shielding covers an insulator, which covers two inner conductors | Usually used in short length and cheap 10G connections. It offers lower latency than usual twisted |
