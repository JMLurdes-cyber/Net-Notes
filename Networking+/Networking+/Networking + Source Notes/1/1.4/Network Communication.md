### CONCEPTS
Understanding the different models of communication through the use of Unicast, multicast

### KEY TAKEAWAYS
| Connection | Funciton                                                                                                                                     | Example                                                                      |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unicast    | One-to-one transmission between systems. It is not optimal for multiple simultaneous communications. IPv4 and IPv6                           | Simple connection between devices                                            |
| Multicast  | One-to-many-of-many communication. Multiple recipients will get the sent information at once. It is not used between networks. IPv4 and IPv6 | Multimedia delivery or stock exchanges                                       |
| Anycast    | One-to-one-of-many communication, sends information to the anycast and the closest                                                           | Content Delivery Networks, Distributed Denial of Service protection Services |
| Broadcast  | One to all send, simply gets the package and sends it to all connected devices. Not used in IPv6                                             | Routing updates                                                              |
