Firewalls are devices that ==filter data based on port and protocol.== They are usually installed at the ==innermost or outermost position of the network==. There are two kinds:

- ==Traditional== - ==Filter data based on ports and protocols. Can perform basic routing and VPNs==
- ==Next Generation FireWall== - ==Adds context-based filtering and deep packet inspection and real time scanning. Can perform basic routing and VPNs==
