Switches are devices that ==forward data within a LAN by learning and using the MAC address table==. There are two kinds:
- Layer ==2== switches: ==They forward frames based on MAC addresses. They cannot route between networks or VLANs==
- Layer ==3== switches: ==They combine switching and routing functions, using both MAC and IP addresses to perform inter VLAN routing and connect multiple networks==.
