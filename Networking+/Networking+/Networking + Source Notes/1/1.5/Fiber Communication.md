Understanding the uses of fiber communications alongside the different types of connectors used, such as multi-mode fiber and single-mode fiber


## Key Concepts
*Fiber Connections* are communications transmitted through light, instead of cabling. They are used most effectively for long range communications, since the quality of connection worsens after a longer distance than cabling, however, since there is no physical media, most of the tools used to troubleshoot copper are not useful on it.

There are different kinds of fiber connections:

| Connection        | Function                    | Description                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Multimode Fiber   | Shorter range communication | A communication used by an inexpensive light source, such as led, that communicate simultaneously.                                                                    |
| Single-mode Fiber | Long range communcation     | A communication used by a single and really strong lights source, such as a laser, that communicates in a single connection, up to 100 km without need to be repeated |
